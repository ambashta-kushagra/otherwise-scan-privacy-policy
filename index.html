Privacy Policy

Otherwise Scan · Last updated 22 October 2026

The short version

Your scans stay on your phone. We never receive them, and the app has no accounts, ads or tracking.

About this app

Otherwise Scan is a document scanner made by Otherwise (Kushagra Ambashta). This page explains what happens to your information.

What the app does with your documents

  • When you scan a document or import a picture, the app saves the pages and a PDF in its private storage on your phone.
  • The app reads the text on each page (OCR) and saves that text on your phone so you can search it.
  • Scanning and text reading happen on your phone, using Google Play services (ML Kit).
  • You can rename or delete a document at any time. Deleting removes its pages, PDF and text from the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your scans, your text, or any personal information. The app does not send your documents to any server.

Sharing

A document leaves the app only when you tap Share and pick where to send it. After that, the app you choose handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions of its own. The scanner screen is provided by Google Play services, which handles the camera for you.

Google Play services

The scanner is provided by Google Play services. Google may need to download the scanner module once, so an internet connection can be needed the first time you scan. Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to Google Play services: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your control

All your data is on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
